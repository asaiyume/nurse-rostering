Generate a nurse roster directly from the supplied JSON instance. Do not write or execute code, call tools, or invoke a scheduling solver. Return one JSON object only, without Markdown fences or surrounding commentary.

Hard constraints:
1. Give each nurse exactly one record per listed day, including Off. A nurse works at most one shift per day.
2. Meet every minimum coverage requirement for each day, shift, and skill.
3. A working record covers exactly one skill that the nurse possesses. Multi-skilled nurses cannot fill two coverage requirements in one shift.
4. Respect forbidden_successions between consecutive days, including the history last_shift to Monday. Off breaks a working-shift succession.

Soft objectives for this pilot, after satisfying all hard constraints:
- Each nurse missing from preferred coverage costs 30. Extra coverage has no penalty.
- Each violated shift_off_request costs 10. Any requests the whole day off; a named shift requests only that shift off.
- If a nurse's contract has complete_weekends=true, working exactly one of Saturday/Sunday costs 30.
Minimise the sum of these three penalties. The remaining contract and consecutive-day fields are retained for future experiments but are not enforced as hard constraints or scored in this pilot. Do not apply four/eight-week total limits as one-week limits.
